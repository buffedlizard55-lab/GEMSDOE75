When an upload validator checks (arr >= 0.0) & (arr <= 1.0) across the full 3730 x 3292 array without masking NaN, IEEE-754 evaluates NaN >= 0.0 as False and rejects the file.

How submissions/gemsdoe75-h75-strike-corrob-b2-d285-k38200-20261021-zero.tif is 100% immune: All 58,171 sentinel entries are scrubbed to NaN and median-imputed inside the footprint in scripts/prepare_data.py, and vendor/gems57/submission_writer.py writes 0.0f outside the footprint with nodata = None, verifying on disk that all 12,279,160 pixels are finite numbers in [0.0, 1.0] (n_nan = 0, n_infinite = 0, min = 0.0, max = 1.0).
