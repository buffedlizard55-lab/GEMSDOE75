WHAT WE LEARNED

Three findings worth carrying forward.

01

Geometry is not discovery.

Sparse placement can help distance-tolerant coverage. H33’s hidden-score causation is unproven; a fault map does not establish a geothermal vent.

02

Novelty needs a definition.

Continuous support saturates the inherited overlap gate. A low rank correlation does not cancel a failed overlap test.

03

A negative result is useful.

No renamed prior. No invented score. No wasted submission slot. The blockers, audit and next experiments are preserved.

Four hypotheses. No promised score.

Ranked physical mechanisms, named mimics, data dependencies, and a preregistered validation plan.
