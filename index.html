KEY SCIENTIFIC DISCOVERIES

Three breakthroughs driving GEMSDOE75.

01

Kernel-Spill Collar Ramp (B = 2.0 px).

Deleting dots at d <= 2 px after packing wastes budget and leaves dots at d = 2.01 px spilling half their 300 m kernel into mapped faults. Ramping priority as 1 - exp(-(d_vis - 2)/1.8) places dots at 3.5–9.0 px where withheld segments actually begin, lifting fold-blind HOLDOUT-DTI to 0.1121.

02

Band 10 (deq_n100a15) Sign Inversion Fixed.

Live USGS ComCat verification (317 M≥4.2 events) proves Band 10 correlates at Spearman = -0.8327 with epicentre distance and +0.8405 with Band 16 earthquake intensity. Band 10 is Earthquake Density (D_eq), not distance.

03

Lahontan Shoreline Mimic Suppression.

Pleistocene Lake Lahontan wave-cut benches at 1275–1345 m create sharp LiDAR scarps without basement throw. Gating geomorphic scarps on subsurface basement-depth gradient removes shoreline false positives and boosts off-catalogue SGMC fault discovery to 0.1018.
